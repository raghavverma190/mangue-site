What Stock Hush uses, and why

Holdings you enter

You add each holding yourself: the ticker, how many shares, and the price you paid. You also choose an alert threshold. Stock Hush uses these to compare today's price to what you paid, so it can tell you whether a holding has moved past your threshold.

Screen Time (the apps you shield)

Stock Hush uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity) to shield the investing apps you choose. You pick those apps in Apple's own Screen Time picker. Your Screen Time selections stay on your device and are never accessible to us. We can't see which apps you chose or how you use them.

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold through Apple's App Store using In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your card or billing details.
