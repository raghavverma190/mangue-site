Common questions

How does Stock Hush know what I hold?

You add your holdings yourself: the ticker, how many shares, and what you paid. Stock Hush compares today's price to that.

Can it lock me out of my brokerage app?

No. If you open an app Stock Hush is shielding, the shield turns off automatically. Stock Hush never locks you out.

How often do prices update?

Stock Hush aims to check every 70 minutes. If more time than that has passed, opening the app is what triggers the refresh.

What happens when my free trial ends?

You choose a plan to keep Stock Hush watching. Cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged. Manage or cancel in your App Store subscriptions.

Is this financial advice?

No. Stock Hush only tells you whether a holding has moved past the threshold you set. It doesn't recommend buying or selling anything.
